Who We Are

A trading post in the Tucson tradition.

Firearms, ammunition, NFA items and antique and collectable pieces — offered privately, by appointment, from Tucson’s east side.

As a home-based business, Tucson Equestrian Trading Post keeps things simple: you call, you set a time, and you deal directly with the owner. There are no counters to wait at and no sales floor to navigate — just straightforward conversation and personal attention.

Orders are taken by phone, and transfers are handled for both standard firearms and machineguns.
